# Credits: luxe set (lx-*)

Checked 14 Oct 2026. All sources are public domain (CC0 or Public Domain Mark / PD on Wikimedia Commons).
We cut them out, toned them and composited them in Chromium canvas (colour key, edge flood fill or texture mask, 1px feather, WebP).
The carpet backgrounds are our own patchworks of tilted crops from several rug photos, gradient-mapped to black/ivory/gold or black/indigo/silver.
